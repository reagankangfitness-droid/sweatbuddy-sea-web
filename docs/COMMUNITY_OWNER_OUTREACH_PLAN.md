## What To Track

- Owner replies
- Claim/update clicks
- Correction requests
- Removal requests
- Outbound join clicks
- Saves
- Reports outdated
- Repeat visits to the directory

## Decision Criteria After 30 Days

Continue if:

- users click outbound join links repeatedly
- users save communities
- owners respond positively or claim/update pages
- reports outdated are low relative to clicks
- one or two categories clearly outperform the rest

Tighten or pivot if:

- users browse but do not click join links
- owners dislike being listed
- listings go stale quickly
- the directory feels like a generic link list
- no category shows repeated demand
